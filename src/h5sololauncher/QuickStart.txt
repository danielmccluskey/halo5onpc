h5sololauncher 0.9.1 preview

Open h5sololauncher.exe. Keep this entire folder together.

Choose an existing complete cache and click Play. No campaign dump is needed.
To build or repair a cache, choose your extracted Halo 5: Guardians dump and a
cache destination, then click Prepare & play. Setup runs automatically.
Next time, click Play. There is no timed Continue or Solo click during setup.
In Solo, choose Osiris or Blue Team, a difficulty and Start New Mission.

Pause keeps completed preparation stages. Stop monitoring and closing the
launcher leave Forge running. Options and diagnostics contains Prepare cache only
for rechecking a cache. Copy details keeps the information needed for bug reports.
Local logs are in %LOCALAPPDATA%\h5sololauncher\logs.

Requires installed Forge 1.194.6192.2 x64 and the Halo companion app.
Building or repairing requires an extracted Guardians 1.1.31695.21 dump. English (US) is the supported voice language.
Choose a writable local NTFS cache outside the dump and Forge installation.
No Python, compiler, Xbox SDK or separate runtime installation is needed.
Game files are not included. Copy the whole cache when moving to another PC,
then select its new location. Paths and Forge read permissions update automatically.
Complete 0.8 caches upgrade automatically; an index-only cache cannot be played.

Validation status: automatic startup and a rendered Osiris starting scene have
been checked. Player controls, Blue Team and the onward mission transition have
not been verified in this build. This preview is not a fully validated replacement
for the old launcher. No later campaign missions are prepared by this release.
